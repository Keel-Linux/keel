# 2026-10-09: web-2 (BR2) and the members' channel (keel#119)

Real nodes, keel 0.23.7. web-1 (CT 124) and web-3 (CT 125) are on site
BR1. web-2 is on site BR2. Only read commands and diagnostics were run:
ping, `ip`, `journalctl`, `keel.mesh.memberlink.exchange` as a client,
and a Python AF_PACKET capture of 200 s on wg0 and eth0 (tcpdump is not
installed, and an install is a change). No configuration was changed.
Public uplink addresses are `<uplink-web1>` and `<uplink-web2>`. The
router that sent the ICMPv6 is `<router>`.

## Result

The cause is the MTU of wg0, not the timeouts and not the RTT.

- web-1 and web-3 have wg0 MTU 1420. web-2 has wg0 MTU 1412. keel does
  not write an MTU line, so wg-quick takes the MTU of the route to an
  endpoint less 80.
- The path from BR1 to BR2 is 1492 bytes (PPPoE at BR2).
- WireGuard pads each packet to a multiple of 16 bytes, up to the MTU of
  its own interface. web-2 sends MSS 1352, so a full segment from web-1
  is 1412 bytes inside, padded to 1420, and 1500 bytes on the wire. The
  path drops it.
- The kernel then needs an ICMPv6 "packet too big" to fragment. The
  route's PMTU entry expires after 10 minutes. In the failure below, the
  ICMPv6 came 26.6 s after the first lost packet, and web-2 stopped at
  its 30 s answer timeout before the first fragmented packet.

## Timeouts against decision 0050

`keel.mesh.memberlink`: CONNECT_TIMEOUT 10 s, ANSWER_TIMEOUT 30 s. The
members' channel is plain HTTP over wg0 (WireGuard is its proof), not
TLS. At 250 ms RTT with 2% loss a 4 KiB answer needs a few RTTs and a
few retransmits of 200 ms to 1.6 s. 30 s is sufficient. Here the RTT
is 4 ms and the answer still did not arrive: a longer timeout or more
retries do not repair a packet that the path always drops.

## Fix and test

keel renders `MTU = 1280` in the wg0 file (keel.network.wireguard,
OVERLAY_MTU). 1280 is the minimum MTU of IPv6, 1360 bytes on the wire
over IPv6, the same on every node. A change of the MTU line bounces
wg0 one time.

`tests/test_network_overlay_mtu_netns.py` reproduces the failure: node
B behind a veth of 1492 bytes that drops longer packets and sends no
ICMPv6, a 4 KiB answer over TCP through wg0, a deadline of 5 s.

```
before the fix (0.23.7 file):  received 0 of 4096 bytes in 5.01 s, wg0 mtu 1420 (A) and 1412 (B)
with the fix:                  received 4096 bytes, wg0 mtu 1280 on A and B
```
